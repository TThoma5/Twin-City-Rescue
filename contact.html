Service Area

Twin Cities Animal Rescue serves the seven-county Minneapolis–Saint Paul metropolitan area, including:

  • Anoka, Carver, Dakota, Hennepin, Ramsey, Scott, and Washington counties.
  • Adoption and foster placements within a 60-mile radius of downtown Minneapolis.
  • Volunteer opportunities available throughout the metro, with remote roles open statewide.

Office Hours
