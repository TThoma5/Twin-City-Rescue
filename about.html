Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue began in 2014 as a small group of neighbors responding to a simple need: too many healthy, adoptable animals were being overlooked in crowded shelters across the Minneapolis–Saint Paul metro. What started with a handful of foster homes and a borrowed transport van has grown into a trusted community rescue serving thousands of animals each year.

Today, we operate almost entirely through a network of dedicated foster families and volunteers. Rather than housing animals in a single facility, we place them in real homes where they can heal, relax, and show their true personalities while they wait for a permanent family. This home-based model lets us say yes to animals who need a little extra time, medical care, or patience before they are ready for adoption.

Our Core Values

Everything we do is guided by a set of shared commitments that keep our work focused, compassionate, and accountable to the animals and community we serve.

  • Compassion First

    We treat every animal and every person with patience, dignity, and respect, recognizing that each arrives with their own story.

  • Community at the Center

    We believe rescue is a shared responsibility, and we work to make involvement accessible to neighbors from all backgrounds.

  • Transparency and Trust

    We are open about how donations are used, how decisions are made, and how animals are matched with families.

  • Responsible Stewardship

    We commit our resources where they do the most good, prioritizing the health and well-being of the animals in our care.

Partner Acknowledgments

Our work would not be possible without the generosity of local organizations that share our commitment to animal welfare. We are proud to recognize the partners who help us rescue, care for, and rehome animals across the Twin Cities.

  • Riverbend Veterinary Clinic — discounted medical care for rescued animals.
  • Northstar Pet Supply — ongoing donations of food, bedding, and supplies.
  • Saint Paul Community Foundation — grants supporting our foster program.
  • Lakeshore Animal Hospital — emergency and surgical care for critical cases.
  • Metro Transit Pet Partners — transport support for inter-shelter transfers.
  • Friends of Minneapolis Shelters — collaborative adoption events and outreach.

We are deeply grateful to every volunteer, foster family, donor, and partner who makes this work possible.
