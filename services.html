How We Help Animals and Our Community

Twin Cities Animal Rescue offers three core pathways for community members to engage with our mission. Whether you are ready to bring a pet home, open your space temporarily, or share your time, each program is designed to match the right animal with the right person at the right moment.

Adoption Process Overview

Our adoption program is built to create lasting, successful matches between people and pets. Every adopter is guided through a clear, supportive process from first interest to bringing their new companion home.

  1. Browse Available Pets

    Review profiles of animals currently in our foster network, including their personality, needs, and ideal home environment.

  2. Submit an Inquiry

    Complete our contact form to express interest in a specific animal or ask general adoption questions.

  3. Meet and Greet

    Schedule a supervised visit with the pet and their foster family to ensure a good fit for your household.

  4. Finalize the Adoption

    Complete the adoption agreement, review care guidance, and welcome your new companion home with ongoing support from our team.

Foster Program Overview

Fosters are the heart of our rescue. By opening your home temporarily, you give an animal a safe, nurturing place to rest, recover, and prepare for adoption while freeing space for another pet in need.

What Fostering Involves

  • Providing daily care, food, and affection in a home setting.
  • Transporting pets to scheduled veterinary appointments as needed.
  • Sharing updates and photos to help match the pet with adopters.
  • Committing to a flexible timeframe, from a few days to several months.

What We Provide

  • All food, supplies, and medical care at no cost to the foster family.
  • Ongoing guidance and support from our foster coordinators.
  • Training resources for first-time fosters.

Volunteer Role Descriptions

Volunteers power nearly every part of our work. We offer a range of roles so that people with different schedules, skills, and comfort levels can all find a meaningful way to contribute.

Event and Adoption Day Volunteer

Help set up and run community adoption events, greet visitors, and assist with pet handling and introductions. Ideal for outgoing volunteers who enjoy working with the public.

Animal Care Volunteer

Support daily care routines including feeding, walking, and socialization for animals in our short-term holding spaces. A great fit for hands-on animal lovers.

Transport Volunteer

Drive animals between foster homes, veterinary clinics, and partner shelters using your own vehicle. Flexible scheduling based on your availability.

Administrative and Outreach Volunteer

Assist with social media, donor communications, data entry, and community outreach from home or at our office. Perfect for those who prefer behind-the-scenes support.
