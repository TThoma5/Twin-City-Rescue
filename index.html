Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a community-driven nonprofit dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming companion animals across the greater Minneapolis–Saint Paul metro area. We believe every animal deserves a safe home, every volunteer deserves a meaningful role, and every neighbor deserves the chance to be part of the solution. Through foster care, adoption, and community partnerships, we connect vulnerable animals with the people and resources they need to thrive.

Our Impact

Since our founding, our volunteers, foster families, and donors have helped us make a measurable difference for animals and the people who love them:

  • More than 2,400 animals placed into loving, permanent homes.
  • Over 650 active foster families caring for pets in transition.
  • More than 18,000 volunteer hours logged in the past year alone.
  • Partnerships with 30 local shelters, clinics, and community organizations.
  • A 96 percent live-release rate for animals entering our program.

How You Can Help

Rescue is a team effort, and there is a place for everyone in our community. Whether you have time, a spare room, or resources to share, your involvement directly shapes an animal's second chance at a happy life.

  • Adopt: Give a rescue pet a permanent, loving home.
  • Foster: Open your home temporarily to a pet in need.
  • Volunteer: Lend your time at events, transports, or care shifts.
  • Donate: Help cover food, medical care, and shelter costs.
  • Partner: Connect your business or organization with our mission.

How We Help Animals and Our Community

Twin Cities Animal Rescue offers three core pathways for community members to engage with our mission. Whether you are ready to bring a pet home, open your space temporarily, or share your time, each program is designed to match the right animal with the right person at the right moment.

Adoption Process Overview

Our adoption program is built to create lasting, successful matches between people and pets. Every adopter is guided through a clear, supportive process from first interest to bringing their new companion home.

  1. Browse Available Pets

    Review profiles of animals currently in our foster network, including their personality, needs, and ideal home environment.

  2. Submit an Inquiry

    Complete our contact form to express interest in a specific animal or ask general adoption questions.

  3. Meet and Greet

    Schedule a supervised visit with the pet and their foster family to ensure a good fit for your household.

  4. Finalize the Adoption

    Complete the adoption agreement, review care guidance, and welcome your new companion home with ongoing support from our team.

Begin your adoption inquiry →

Foster Program Overview

Fosters are the heart of our rescue. By opening your home temporarily, you give an animal a safe, nurturing place to rest, recover, and prepare for adoption while freeing space for another pet in need.

What Fostering Involves

  • Providing daily care, food, and affection in a home setting.
  • Transporting pets to scheduled veterinary appointments as needed.
  • Sharing updates and photos to help match the pet with adopters.
  • Committing to a flexible timeframe, from a few days to several months.

What We Provide

  • All food, supplies, and medical care at no cost to the foster family.
  • Ongoing guidance and support from our foster coordinators.
  • Training resources for first-time fosters.

Volunteer Role Descriptions

Volunteers power nearly every part of our work. We offer a range of roles so that people with different schedules, skills, and comfort levels can all find a meaningful way to contribute.

Event and Adoption Day Volunteer

Help set up and run community adoption events, greet visitors, and assist with pet handling and introductions. Ideal for outgoing volunteers who enjoy working with the public.

Animal Care Volunteer

Support daily care routines including feeding, walking, and socialization for animals in our short-term holding spaces. A great fit for hands-on animal lovers.

Transport Volunteer

Drive animals between foster homes, veterinary clinics, and partner shelters using your own vehicle. Flexible scheduling based on your availability.

Administrative and Outreach Volunteer

Assist with social media, donor communications, data entry, and community outreach from home or at our office. Perfect for those who prefer behind-the-scenes support.

Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue began in 2014 as a small group of neighbors responding to a simple need: too many healthy, adoptable animals were being overlooked in crowded shelters across the Minneapolis–Saint Paul metro. What started with a handful of foster homes and a borrowed transport van has grown into a trusted community rescue serving thousands of animals each year.

Today, we operate almost entirely through a network of dedicated foster families and volunteers. Rather than housing animals in a single facility, we place them in real homes where they can heal, relax, and show their true personalities while they wait for a permanent family. This home-based model lets us say yes to animals who need a little extra time, medical care, or patience before they are ready for adoption.

Our Core Values

Everything we do is guided by a set of shared commitments that keep our work focused, compassionate, and accountable to the animals and community we serve.

  • Compassion First

    We treat every animal and every person with patience, dignity, and respect, recognizing that each arrives with their own story.

  • Community at the Center

    We believe rescue is a shared responsibility, and we work to make involvement accessible to neighbors from all backgrounds.

  • Transparency and Trust

    We are open about how donations are used, how decisions are made, and how animals are matched with families.

  • Responsible Stewardship

    We commit our resources where they do the most good, prioritizing the health and well-being of the animals in our care.

Partner Acknowledgments

Our work would not be possible without the generosity of local organizations that share our commitment to animal welfare. We are proud to recognize the partners who help us rescue, care for, and rehome animals across the Twin Cities.

  • Riverbend Veterinary Clinic — discounted medical care for rescued animals.
  • Northstar Pet Supply — ongoing donations of food, bedding, and supplies.
  • Saint Paul Community Foundation — grants supporting our foster program.
  • Lakeshore Animal Hospital — emergency and surgical care for critical cases.
  • Metro Transit Pet Partners — transport support for inter-shelter transfers.
  • Friends of Minneapolis Shelters — collaborative adoption events and outreach.

We are deeply grateful to every volunteer, foster family, donor, and partner who makes this work possible.

Service Area

Twin Cities Animal Rescue serves the seven-county Minneapolis–Saint Paul metropolitan area, including:

  • Anoka, Carver, Dakota, Hennepin, Ramsey, Scott, and Washington counties.
